ACME ROBOTICS — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (PUBLIC)

Welcome to Acme Robotics. This handbook covers general workplace policies.

Working hours are 9am to 5pm, Monday through Friday. Remote work is permitted
two days per week with manager approval.

Paid time off accrues at 1.5 days per month. Requests should be submitted at
least two weeks in advance through the HR portal.

The company observes all federal holidays. The office is closed between
December 24 and January 1.

For IT support, contact the help desk at extension 4357. For HR questions,
email people@acme-robotics.example.

ACME ROBOTICS — PRODUCT FAQ (PUBLIC)

Q: What does the Acme R100 do?
A: The R100 is a warehouse automation robot for palletizing and transport.

Q: What is the battery life?
A: The R100 runs for approximately 8 hours on a full charge and recharges in 2.

Q: Is the R100 safe around people?
A: Yes. The R100 uses lidar and proximity sensors to stop within 10cm of an
obstacle. It is rated for collaborative operation.

Q: How do I get support?
A: Contact support@acme-robotics.example or call 1-800-555-0100.

Q: What is the warranty?
A: The R100 includes a two-year limited warranty on parts and labor.
